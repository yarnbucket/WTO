# What’s the Odds?

What’s the Odds? helps you follow NFL matchups, compare team trends, and understand the betting lines. A spread is the expected winning margin; a total is the expected combined score.

Save your selections in **My Pick Slip** to track how you do. Odds refresh Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday, while your saved lines stay fixed.

Coming next: simple weekly graphs comparing your picks, WTO predictions, market lines, and actual results.

Want a game-day atmosphere? Turn on **Atmosphere** in Settings. It’s optional and off by default. Effects appear only inside the selected matchup when a forecast is available.

## October 11, 2026 developments

- Settings → Theme now offers Classic WTO, Vintage Gridiron, Halloween Gridiron, and Christmas Gridiron. Classic remains the default; the device remembers the selected theme.
- Vintage uses Times New Roman; Halloween uses Creepster headings with readable body text; Christmas uses Georgia headings, evergreen, cream and gold.
- Seasonal summary panels and matchup cards retain the existing 40% background opacity. Text and controls remain legible; transparency applies to backgrounds rather than the entire card.
- Percentage meters now have transparent football-field borders, yard numbers, and hash marks. Each advances from its own left end zone toward the opposite label: OVER → UNDER, UNDER → OVER, FAV → DOG, DOG → FAV. Labels are enlarged and centered; 50% is midfield. End zones are outside the measured playing area.
- Team Info has a compact logo/name header with division and mascot together underneath.
- Optional Atmosphere introduces rain/storm with fine branching lightning, sunshine with a short glow, and snow/sleet with a small squall from the weather badge. Intro effects fade, leaving subtle ongoing weather. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation; unavailable/indoor forecasts do not start effects.
- Picks, custom lines, prediction history, results and tracking are preserved when themes change.

## Installed-app updates

The service worker checks for updates at launch, when the app becomes visible, and when connectivity returns. A versioned shell cache refreshes app files without clearing saved picks or theme preferences. Open clients show an in-app **Update now** notice when a newer worker takes control. After loading this release, a one-time **WTO updated** notice describes the additions.

Older clients with the previous automatic-refresh handler may reload once to receive this notice. Closed/offline apps receive the update when they next open online; this is an in-app notification, not an operating-system push notification.
